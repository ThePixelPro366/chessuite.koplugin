Maia3 5M by the University of Toronto CSSLab.
Code: https://github.com/CSSLab/maia3 (AGPL-3.0); tools/maia3/maia3.c is a C port of its inference.
Weights: https://huggingface.co/UofTCSSLab/Maia3-5M (maia3-5m.pt), converted to half precision
with tools/export-maia3.py into maia3-5m.bin.
Paper: Chessformer: A Unified Architecture for Chess Modeling, https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19091
